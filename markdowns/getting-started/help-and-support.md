# Help & Support

> Update your site developer's contact details and notification preferences, find resources, or contact the plugin developer.

## Page Navigation

- **Where to open it:** **ArraySubs -> Help**
- **Direct admin route:** `/wp-admin/admin.php?page=arraysubs-mainadmin#/help`
- **Section overview:** [Getting Started](README.md)
- **Related settings:** [Easy Setup & Import Export](import-export-settings.md)

## Page Layout

The Help page has three rows. On desktop, the first and third rows span the page width. The middle row has two cards side by side. On phones, the cards stack in the same reading order.

### 1. Site Developer and Notification Preferences

The first row shows your site's developer or maintainer, their email address and notification preferences. This is the person responsible for maintaining your website.

1. Click the edit icon beside the recipient to change the name or email address.
2. Confirm the recipient with the checkmark.
3. Select the notifications you want: **Security & Updates**, occasional offers, or articles about case studies and ecommerce marketing.
4. Click **Save Notification Preferences**. The button is enabled when there are unsaved changes and the recipient editor is closed.

All three notification choices are optional. Clearing every choice removes the recipient from the notification subscriber list after saving.

**Contact site developer** copies the recipient's email address and opens your email app. This action contacts your site's maintainer.

### 2. Features & Updates and Guides & Resources

The second row contains two cards:

| Card | Links |
| --- | --- |
| **Features & Updates** | **Urgent Feature Request** opens the contact form. **Roadmap & Changelog** opens the product roadmap. |
| **Guides & Resources** | **User Documentation** opens this manual. **Developer Documentation** opens the [developer guides](../developers/README.md). **Visit Plugin Website** opens the plugin website. |

These links open in a new tab. On phones, Features & Updates appears before Guides & Resources.

### 3. Contact the Plugin Developer Directly

The final row is for setup questions, bug reports and other plugin support. Click **Contact Plugin Developer Directly** to open the ArrayHash contact form in a new tab.

The secondary **Check Developer's Doc** button opens the [developer documentation](../developers/README.md) in a new tab for PHP APIs, lifecycle hooks, actions, and filters.

Include the affected page, what you tried, what you expected and what happened. If the question concerns a subscription or order, include its ID so the issue can be traced.
